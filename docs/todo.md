# Deferred topics

This list is non-normative. Its contents are not supported or required by the MVP.

- Quantity representation, units, and validation.
- Structured uncertainty, conflicting claims, ranking, deprecation, and historical claim lifecycle.
- Duplicate identity resolution, aliases, redirects, merges, and tombstones.
- Concurrent identifier allocation and merge-conflict handling.
- Evidence excerpts and passage or page locators.
- Separating stable source identities from retrieval captures.
- Qualifier-specific provenance.
- Ontology-evolution and migration tooling.
